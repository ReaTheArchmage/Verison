type, definition_divisor, name, args, end_of_definition

verison:
type:               whatever in brackets left to def_div.
definition_divisor: first occurence of : from the start
name:               whatever to the left of def_div untill (
args:               whatever in parenthesis. If divided by , means different elements
end_of_definition:  last character in line
